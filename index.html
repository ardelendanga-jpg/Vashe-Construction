Who we are

Construction that understands the people who'll use it

Vashe Construction is a Zimbabwean construction and infrastructure services company delivering practical, quality solutions for agricultural, residential, commercial, institutional and community projects.

Good construction isn't only about putting up buildings — it's about creating safe, functional, durable and inclusive spaces that respond to the people who use them. Our approach is client-focused, cost-conscious and quality-driven, from the first site visit to final handover.

Our vision

To become a trusted construction and infrastructure partner delivering quality, affordable, sustainable and inclusive built environments across Zimbabwe and the region.

Our mission

To provide professional construction, renovation and infrastructure solutions that meet client needs while upholding quality, safety, accessibility and environmental responsibility.

Our service promise

  • Quality — built to last
  • Affordable — designed around practical budgets
  • Inclusive — accessible to people of all abilities
  • Practical — designed around the client's needs
  • Reliable — delivered professionally
  • Sustainable — built for long-term value

  • Plastering, rendering & painting

How a project runs

A practical, six-step delivery process

  1. 01

    Assessment

    We visit the site, understand your requirements and assess existing infrastructure where applicable.

  2. 02

    Planning & costing

    We develop a scope of works, quantities, materials requirements and cost estimates.

  3. 03

    Design & technical planning

    Where required, we work with qualified technical professionals on designs, drawings and specifications.

  4. 04

    Construction

    Our teams coordinate materials, labour and site activity while maintaining quality and safety standards.

  5. 05

    Quality control

    We monitor workmanship and materials throughout to ensure works meet agreed specifications.

  6. 06

    Handover

    Completed works are inspected with you and formally handed over on completion.
